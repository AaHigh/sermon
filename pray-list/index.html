We Bring These Names

A private intercession for Shelly Walker, Debbie Willems, Isaiah Tristan, Ron DeSantiago, Gina Carsten, the family and friends of Don Velasquez, and for the world.

Father in heaven, we come in the name of Your Son, the Lord Jesus Christ. We do not come to perform. We come to ask. We name the people You already know. We ask for what only You can give. Amen.

1 Timothy 2:1-2

I urge you, first of all, to pray for all people. Ask God to help them; intercede on their behalf, and give thanks for them. Pray this way for kings and all who are in authority so that we can live peaceful and quiet lives marked by godliness and dignity.

Paul puts intercession first. Not last. Not after the noise. First. We bring names because the Father does not deal in crowds alone. He deals in persons. He numbers the hairs. He knows the need before we speak it. Still He invites us to speak it.

James 5:16

Confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed. The earnest prayer of a righteous person has great power and produces wonderful results.

Earnest prayer. Not polished. Not long for the sake of length. Earnest. We bring these names in that spirit.

Shelly Walker. We ask You to meet her where she is. Give her strength for whatever weight she carries. Give her wisdom for the decisions that sit in front of her. Guard her heart and mind. Let Your peace that exceeds understanding settle on her. If there is healing she needs, body or spirit, supply it according to Your will. Keep her near the truth that sets free.

Debbie Willems. We lift her name. Cover her with Your protection. Supply what she lacks. Steady her steps. If anxiety or sorrow presses, replace it with the peace that guards. Let her know she is seen. Let the people around her be instruments of Your care. Keep her rooted in Christ.

Isaiah Tristan. We pray for this one. Give him courage. Give him clarity. Protect him from the traps that hunt the young and the old alike. If he needs direction, open the path. If he needs correction, make it clear and kind. Let him hear the voice of the Shepherd more than the noise of the street. Hold him close.

Ron DeSantiago. We bring Ron before You. Strengthen his body and his resolve. If he is fighting something, fight with him. If he needs rest, give true rest. Surround him with people who tell the truth and who stand with him. Let the name of Jesus be a strong tower for him today and every day.

Gina Carsten. We name Gina. Meet her needs. Heal what is broken. Comfort what is grieving. Guide what is confused. Let Your Word light her path. Keep her from isolation. Put the right voices in her ear and the right hands on her shoulder. Let her finish the race set before her with faith intact.

Don Velasquez. He has left this life. We do not pretend the loss is light. We pray for his family and his friends. Comfort the ones who wake up to the empty chair. Carry the ones who feel the weight of unfinished words. Give them the hope that is not wishful thinking but the resurrection of Jesus Christ. Let the memory of Don be held with honesty and with mercy. Bind up the brokenhearted. Teach the living to number their days so they gain a heart of wisdom.

Psalm 34:18

The Lord is close to the brokenhearted; he rescues those whose spirits are crushed.

And we pray for the world. Not the slogan world. The actual world of people and powers and cities and nations. We ask for mercy on a generation that loves darkness more than light. We ask for leaders who fear God more than they fear the next headline. We ask for churches that tell the truth instead of managing optics. We ask for the gospel to run free. We ask for the poor to be protected and the predators to be stopped. We ask for the peace that only comes when men and women turn to the living God.

Philippians 4:6-7

Don’t worry about anything; instead, pray about everything. Tell God what you need, and thank him for all he has done. Then you will experience God’s peace, which exceeds anything we can understand. His peace will guard your hearts and minds as you live in Christ Jesus.

We have named them. We have asked. We leave the outcomes with the One who sees the end from the beginning. We thank Him already for what He will do that we cannot yet see. We thank Him for the blood of Jesus that opens the way for every honest request.

Brothers and sisters who share this private page, keep these names before the Lord as the Spirit leads. Do not turn intercession into a show. Do not use the names for leverage. Simply pray. The Father who sees in secret will answer in the way that fits His glory and their good.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, the Lord Jesus Christ, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

Scripture quotations are from the New Living Translation.
