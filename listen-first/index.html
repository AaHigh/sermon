Let the Lord Have the Floor

A short charge for a brother who cannot stop talking long enough to hear God, and a stepwise path back to the Lord's voice.

Lord, give Johnny ears before You give him more words. Amen.

Johnny, this is not a roast. This is a rescue. You already know the pattern. You take the floor. You keep the floor. The music stays on when a human being is trying to finish a thought. You cut a sentence in half because the next thing in your head feels more important than the person standing in front of you. That is not leadership. That is a man who is afraid of silence.

James 1:19-20

Understand this, my dear brothers and sisters: You must all be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to get angry. Human anger does not produce the righteousness God desires.

The Lord put the order in writing. Listen first. Speak second. Anger last, if at all. You have been running that list backward. You speak first. You listen if there is time. You get hot when someone asks you to wait. That is why the room tightens when you walk in. People do not hate your voice. They are tired of never getting to finish theirs.

Proverbs 18:13

Spouting off before listening to the facts is both shameful and foolish.

Shameful is a hard word. The Book uses it anyway. Interrupting a man mid-sentence is not a personality. It is a small theft. You steal the rest of his thought and you replace it with yours. Playing music over a conversation is the same theft with a soundtrack. You are telling the room that your noise is the main event. God is not impressed by volume.

Some of what you fight may live in the body. Some of it may live in old habits. Some of it may have been in the family before you were born. The Scripture does not mock a weak body. It also does not let a weak body write the rules. A cause is not a license. If the impulse is strong, the obedience has to get stronger, not softer.

Proverbs 25:28

A person without self-control is like a city with broken-down walls.

That is the picture. The gate is open. Every thought walks in and takes the microphone. You do not have to invent a new religion to fix that. Self-control is not a gym trick you grind out alone. It is fruit. Fruit grows where the Spirit is allowed to work. You cannot produce it by talking about it. You receive it by staying close enough for the vine to feed you.

Galatians 5:22-23

But the Holy Spirit produces this kind of fruit in our lives: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. There is no law against these things!

Notice where self-control sits. At the end of a list that starts with love. You do not get the last fruit by skipping the first ones. Love listens. Patience waits. Gentleness does not run over a smaller voice. If you want the Spirit's control, you start by refusing to treat other people as an audience for your soundtrack.

You said you are not sure how to gain access to the Lord's direction. That is an honest sentence. Hear this next one. God is not hiding in a special frequency that only loud men can find. The Shepherd still uses a voice. Sheep learn it by stopping.

John 10:27

My sheep listen to my voice; I know them, and they follow me.

Listening comes before following. You cannot follow a voice you drown out. If the room is always full of your music and your next point, you will call that energy. Heaven will call it noise. The first access point is not a new teacher. It is a shut door and a still body.

Matthew 6:6

But when you pray, go away by yourself, shut the door behind you, and pray to your Father in private. Then your Father, who sees everything, will reward you.

Psalm 46:10

Be still, and know that I am God! I will be honored by every nation. I will be honored throughout the world.

Stillness is not emptiness. Stillness is how a man makes room for a King. You do not have to feel holy first. You have to stop performing long enough for the Father to be the loudest person in the room.

Johnny, here is the stepwise path. Do not skip to step seven because it sounds spiritual. Walk them in order. If you fall, start again the same day. Do not wait for a new month and a new speech.

  1. Name the pattern out loud to God without a defense. Say the words: I interrupt. I dominate. I use music to hold the room. I want to be first. Do not add because. The Lord already knows the because.
  2. When another person is speaking, the music goes off. Not down. Off. If you cannot hear their last word, you are still on the throne.
  3. Give every speaker one full sentence plus a breath. Count it if you have to. You may answer after the breath. You may not answer during the sentence.
  4. Once a day, shut a door for ten minutes. Sit. No phone. No track. Ask one sentence: Lord, what do You want me to hear today? Then wait the whole ten.
  5. Read one short passage from the New Living Translation out loud. James 1:19 is enough for a week. Then close the Book and keep quiet for one minute. That is how a man trains his ears.
  6. Pick one living person this week and refuse to interrupt them. Not a crowd. One person. If you fail, apologize in the same hour. Do not explain. Apologize.
  7. When the old hunger returns, do not preach at it. Take it to the private room and admit weakness. Grace is not a slogan. It is power for a man who finally ran out of his own.

2 Corinthians 12:9

Each time he said, “My grace is all you need. My power works best in weakness.” So now I am glad to boast about my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ can work through me.

Philippians 2:3-4

Don’t be selfish; don’t try to impress others. Be humble, thinking of others as better than yourselves. Don’t look out only for your own interests, but take an interest in others, too.

Humility is not thinking you are trash. Humility is thinking the other man's sentence might matter. Christ had every right to dominate every conversation on earth. He did not. He asked questions. He let people finish. He went away to pray. If the Son of God made room, you can make room.

Philippians 2:13

For God is working in you, giving you the desire and the power to do what pleases him.

That is the access you asked for. Desire and power both come from Him. You bring the obedience of the next ten minutes. He brings the change that lasts longer than a mood. You will not become a quiet saint by tonight. You can become a man who stops mid-interruption and sits back down. That is already closer to God than another speech about how hard it is.

James 1:22

But don’t just listen to God’s word. You must do what it says. Otherwise, you are only fooling yourselves.

James 1:26

If you claim to be religious but don’t control your tongue, you are fooling yourself, and your religion is worthless.

Worthless is another hard word. Keep it. A faith that cannot yield the floor is a costume. The Lord is not asking you to disappear. He is asking you to stop crowding Him out of your own mouth.

Micah 6:8

No, O people, the Lord has told you what is good, and this is what he requires of you: to do what is right, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God.

Walk. Not sprint. Not lecture. Walk humbly. Right, merciful, and quiet enough to notice that God is already speaking. Johnny, the path is open. The next step is small enough to take before supper. Turn the music off. Let the other man finish. Shut the door. Ask the Father. Then keep still until He has the floor.

The charge: Tonight, ten minutes. Door shut. Music off. James 1:19 spoken once. Then silence. Begin there, and do not decorate it.
